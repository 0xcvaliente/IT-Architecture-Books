IT ARCHITECTURE SERIES — DESIGN IDENTITY
Revised edition, October 2026

Reference: a restrained black, white and blue identity inspired by the user-supplied Uber 2018 brand quick guide.
It is an original course layout with no Uber logos and no claim of affiliation.

Type
  Nimbus Sans for body text, headings, tables and captions; DejaVu Sans Mono for code, chosen for clearly
  distinguished 0/O and 1/l/I. Every font slot in the Word styles is set explicitly, so headings never fall
  back to a theme font (Calibri/Carlito).
  Body text: 10 pt, single line spacing. Headings: 22, 12.5 and 10.5 pt bold. Captions 9 pt; tables 8.6 pt;
  code listings 7.6 pt on a pale grey band; inline code one size below the surrounding text.
  Italic marks titles of works and the occasional emphasised word; bold marks a term at its definition.
  The PDF export disables ligatures in the code font, so identifiers such as in_flight print letter by letter.

Palette
  Black #000000, white #FFFFFF, navigational blue #276EF1, pale grey #F3F3F3, dark grey #323232,
  pale blue #E8F0FE for the single emphasised element in a figure.

Page and components
  A4, 2.2 cm side margins. Running header: book title on the left, current chapter title on the right.
  Footer: book number and page number.
  Covers: large blue volume numeral, bold black title, subtitle, and statistics computed from the manuscript.
  Every chapter starts on a new page with a blue "CHAPTER N" label and stays together with its learning
  objectives; back matter (labs, answers, glossary, references, index) also starts on a new page.
  References follow IEEE style; DOI sources print "doi:" with a link and no access date, web sources print
  "Accessed" and "Available".
  Index: two columns, grouped by initial letter (numerals under 0–9), with page numbers taken from the
  checked PDF and terms drawn from the glossary and the bold definitions in the chapters.
  Tables: black header row with white bold text, alternating pale grey rows, header repeated across pages;
  short tables are kept on one page and columns are never narrower than their longest word.
  Code listings never split across a page and are limited to 92 characters per line.
  Callouts ("Common mistake", "In practice", "Check your understanding"): pale grey band with a blue left rule.
  Figures: numbered automatically, captioned in full sentences, referenced in the text as "Figure N".

Figures
  Figures are positioned diagram scenes (authoring/bookN/figures/src/*.scene.json) or hand-written SVG,
  rendered to SVG and 5268 px PNG with DejaVu Sans text in the palette above. The smallest figure text prints
  at 6.8 pt or larger at the 16.3 cm figure width; tall figures are capped at 12.5 cm high. When a figure
  would leave a large gap at the foot of a page, release.py moves it past a few paragraphs or reduces it
  slightly (never below 72 percent) so the text fills the page.

Editing
  The DOCX files are editable in Word. Pagination depends on the installed fonts; the supplied PDFs are the
  checked layout. After content edits, rebuild with authoring/common/release.py so contents and index page
  numbers are recomputed and verified.
