Book 3, Enterprise Architecture: optional copies of the printed listings

These four programs are exact copies of the listings printed in the book. They
use only the Python 3.10+ standard library and print the output shown there.

  investment_model.py      Chapter 10: NPV, IRR, payback, sensitivity and funding cuts
  roadmap_dependencies.py  Chapter 11: critical path and a shared-team constraint
  supplier_sensitivity.py  Chapter 13: mandatory condition, weights and tipping point
  outcome_measurement.py   Chapter 14: Simpson's paradox, standardisation and DiD

enterprise_templates.md repeats the dossier templates printed in "Templates and
further study". All inputs are the book's Helios teaching assumptions.
