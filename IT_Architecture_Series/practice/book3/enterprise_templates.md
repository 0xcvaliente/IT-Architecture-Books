# Enterprise architecture dossier templates

These are the templates printed in "Templates and further study" in Book 3. Copy them into any
document or spreadsheet. Mark a field "unknown" when the information does not yet exist.

## Decision brief

Decision, date and owner:
  (The decision requested, when it is needed and who is accountable)
Problem and scope:
  (What is wrong today, for whom, and what is out of scope)
Recommendation:
  (The proposed option and at least one credible alternative)
Mandatory conditions:
  (Legal, security or continuity conditions every option must meet)
Cost and consequences:
  (Investment, operating cost changes and transition effects)
Uncertainty:
  (What is not yet known and how it will be resolved)
Condition for the next stage:
  (The results that release, narrow or stop further funding)

## Capability assessment

Capability:
  (Identifier, name, definition and level-1 domain)
Importance:
  (High, medium or low, and the strategic objective it supports)
Effectiveness:
  (Score from 1 to 5 and the data behind it)
Confidence:
  (High, medium or low, and the source of the rating)
Supporting elements:
  (Processes, applications and the accountable owner)
Proposed action:
  (Measure, quick fix, investment or no change, with the reason)

## Application inventory record

Application:
  (Identifier, business purpose and accountable owner)
Users and consumers:
  (Interactive users and non-interactive consumers such as batch jobs)
Authority and interfaces:
  (Facts it is authoritative for, interfaces and dependencies)
Cost:
  (Annual operating cost by type: licences, infrastructure, support, shared services)
Lifecycle and contract:
  (Support status, contract end date and exit terms)
Assessment:
  (TIME scores and quadrant, and the proposed disposition)
Gaps:
  (Missing facts and who will find them)

## Fact-authority register

Fact:
  (The business fact or relationship, with its definition)
Authority:
  (The authoritative source and the accountable owner)
Writers and consumers:
  (Who may change it and who relies on it, with freshness needs)
Correction:
  (How disputes and errors are resolved, and by whom)
Policy:
  (Access rules and the source of the retention policy)

## Exception record

Standard:
  (The standard affected and its owner)
Reason and scope:
  (Why compliance is not yet possible and what is covered)
Controls:
  (Compensating controls that are operating now, and conditions still to be met)
Risk owner:
  (Who accepts the residual risk, within which delegation)
Expiry:
  (End date, review trigger and the funded plan to comply)

## Benefits register

Benefit:
  (Identifier, affected population and baseline)
Mechanism:
  (How the change produces the benefit, and its measure and owner)
Type:
  (Cash, capacity or risk reduction)
Timing and cost:
  (When it arrives and what it costs to realise)
Checks:
  (Possible double counting and alternative causes)
Realisation:
  (The data that will show it happened, and the action if it does not)

## Transition state

State:
  (Identifier, entry condition and exit condition)
Authority:
  (The authoritative source and allowed writers for each fact)
Operation:
  (Interfaces, recovery, reconciliation and the support owner)
Temporary elements:
  (Bridges and workarounds, with their retirement condition and budget)
Work:
  (Work packages, predecessors, durations and resource assumptions)
Money and risk:
  (Annual cash need, main risks and the effect of each change card)

## Source log

Claim:
  (The claim the source is used for, and its scope)
Source:
  (Full reference and the kind of source)
Location:
  (Where in the source the support appears)
Design:
  (Method, sample and main limits)
Inference:
  (How it applies to Helios, and the strongest counterargument)
Test:
  (The observation that would confirm or overturn the inference)
